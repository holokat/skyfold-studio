  bundled resource directories retain their source-specific `CREDITS.md`
  files. Those files distinguish photographs, scans, procedural artwork and
  generated artwork. CC0 and public-domain material retains that status.
- **Fish and audio**: Emberfall assets and generated Skyfold audio are
  included with the project owner's permission for public distribution.
  Their bundled `CREDITS.md` files record the sources.
- **Cinzel, EB Garamond and Public Sans**: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  The app includes the font credits and full `assets/fonts/map/OFL.txt`.

Cargo dependencies retain their own licenses. The macOS bundle includes a
dependency notice directory generated from the locked Cargo metadata and the
installed dependency license files.

Paid marketplace packs, editable pack sources and pack generators are excluded
from the public source and app download. Marketplace purchases carry their own
licenses and are delivered separately through the marketplace.
