## New

- **An open sound library.** Every sound in Skyfold Studio is now free to ship and to export into your own projects: CC0 recordings, or synthesised live by the studio (wind, gusts, rain, rivers, waterfalls, surf, fire, lava and cave drips). The thanks to the recordists are in the credits.
- **Shade under the trees.** The sky's light dims under and inside tree crowns, on the ground, grass and plants, while sunlight keeps its shadows. Ferns and broad-leaved herbs grow in clumps under the crowns, and photo grass tufts in the open.
- **Denser forests.** The Trees amount now goes up to 500%.
- **Fly-through has its own button** in the top bar: click places to fly over, preview the flight and capture a video.
- **Preview paid style packs.** The Marketplace shows every model in a paid pack, with its free samples ready to place.

## Improved

- **Faster fields.** Crop fields draw up to about 1.6× faster when you're close to them (a sunflower field edge went from 15 to 24 frames a second). They look exactly the same.
- **Faster forests and skies.** Dense forests draw about 10% faster, and the sky's lighting is only worked out again when the sky changes.
- **Exports look like the editor.** Godot, Unity and Unreal exports now match the editor's weather, clouds, haze, water, meadow grass and wind. Unreal gets a plugin that builds the landscape and runs walk mode, portals and fires.
