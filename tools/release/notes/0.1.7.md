## New

- **Sci-Fi worlds.** File › New world (and the guided setup) now asks for a world type: Fantasy, as before, or Sci-Fi. A Sci-Fi world gets layered alien canyonlands, red-rock ground, giant rock spires, floating rocks and monoliths, alien plants, a sea of cloud in the canyons and a ringed giant planet in the sky. Everything stays editable, and every part is a tool you can use in any world.
- **Planets, moons, distant suns and black holes** in the sky (Lighting › Sky bodies), lit by your world's sun and seen through its air. Ten ready-made **Sci-Fi looks** in Style › Look › Sci-Fi set the sky, the light, what drifts in the air and what the plants look like in one click.
- **Canyonlands and strata.** Sculpt › Canyons raises stepped plateaus, terraced canyons, mesas and lakes at different heights; Sculpt › Strata weathers slopes into rock layers.
- **Colour grading.** One grade for the whole world, your own LUTs, and a round trip through Photoshop.
- **Gameplay cameras.** Over-the-shoulder, first person, classic MMO, isometric and top-down presets, with indoor pull-in and see-through roofs.
- **Lifts** with call buttons and floor displays, sliding and one-way doors, and light fixtures.
- **Crystals in any colour,** each with its own glow and light.
- **City & sci-fi surfaces:** asphalt, pavers, terrazzo, deck plating, concrete, tech panels and more, for the ground and for buildings.
- **A Marketplace window** where creators manage their own listings, with pack previews.

## Improved

- **A smaller download.** The City & sci-fi and Hand-painted surfaces now download the first time you use them.
- **Open sound library.** Ambience is now CC0 recordings and Skyfold's own sound synthesis: live wind, rain, water, surf, fire and underwater.
- **Faster forests and fields,** and the ocean isn't drawn when it's out of sight.
- **Set pieces keep their groups and locks** when you save and reopen a world.
- **MCP connections need a key,** so other apps on your Mac can't drive the editor.
