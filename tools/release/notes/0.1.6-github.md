Skyfold Studio is a free, open-source 3D world builder built with Rust and Bevy.

Download **Skyfold-Studio-macOS.dmg**, open it, and drag Skyfold Studio into Applications. This preview is for Apple silicon Macs. The app is Developer ID signed and the DMG is notarized by Apple.

**Coming from 0.1.2 or earlier?** Download this version once. From 0.1.3 on, Skyfold Studio updates itself.

## Fixed

- **The sky lights the world correctly again.** In 0.1.4 and 0.1.5 the soft light from the sky could be missing or out of date, most visibly at night: a world opened or switched to night could look washed out or too dark. Lighting is back to how it was in 0.1.3; everything else new in 0.1.4 stays.

## Improved

- **Smoother worlds with fish.** Fish schools that are out of view no longer move every frame. They pick up exactly where they should be when you look back.

Worlds, imports and settings stay in `~/Library/Application Support/Skyfold Studio`.
